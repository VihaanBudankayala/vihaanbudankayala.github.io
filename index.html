About the artist

Vihaan Budankayala is an artist interested in how light settles into ordinary rooms, and how a few marks can hold an entire memory.

Trained across traditional and digital media, Vihaan's practice moves between oil studies, ink drawings, and screen-based work — often returning to the same quiet subjects until they give something up. The studio is a working space more than a showroom: pieces here are shown as they develop, not only once they're finished.

This page will grow alongside the work. Replace the plaques above with photographs as pieces are completed, and swap this note for a fuller biography, exhibition history, and CV whenever that's ready.

— V. Budankayala
