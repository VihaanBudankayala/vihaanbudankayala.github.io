About

Vihaan Budankayala is an artist and a student at Argyle High School in Argyle, Texas, Class of 2030. His work starts from close observation and tight line, then moves into bold color and abstract shape.

His subjects include the creatures and wildflowers of the Texas landscape, quiet still lifes, and the festivals and traditions of his Indian heritage. In pen and ink he builds texture one line at a time, from the scales of a horned lizard to the veins of tropical leaves. In graphite he works with soft value and light. In oil pastel and acrylic, saturated color leads.

“I like starting with something I can see, like a lizard, a pineapple or a vase, and finding out how much is really there when I slow down and look.”
